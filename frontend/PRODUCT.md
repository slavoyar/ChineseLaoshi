# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Mandarin learners at any level who want to build vocabulary and character recognition through active practice. They use the app on phones, tablets, and desktops — often in short study sessions between other activities. Touch input matters: handwriting practice is a core mode, not an afterthought.

## Product Purpose

Chinese Laoshi helps users master Mandarin through interactive flashcards and two study regimes: handwriting (trace/write characters) and translation guessing. Users create custom word groups, review new entries, and drill until characters stick. Success means confident recall under pressure — reading, writing, and translating — without the interface getting in the way.

## Brand Personality

Playful, energetic, encouraging. The app should feel like a coach who celebrates progress and keeps momentum high, without talking down to the learner. Study should feel rewarding, not like homework.

## Anti-references

- Childish gamification (cartoon mascots, excessive badges, Duolingo-style streak guilt, babyish color palettes)
- Generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics applied to a learning tool
- Cluttered interfaces that compete with the characters being studied

## Design Principles

1. **Characters first** — Chinese text is the hero on every study screen; chrome stays out of the way.
2. **Momentum over ceremony** — reduce friction between "open app" and "start practicing"; celebrate small wins without blocking the next card.
3. **Touch-native practice** — handwriting mode must feel natural on phone and tablet; hit targets and stroke areas are sized for fingers, not just mouse cursors.
4. **Honest feedback** — wrong answers are clear and kind; right answers feel earned, not patronizing.
5. **Progress you can see** — learners should always know where they are in a session and what comes next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA for text contrast, focus states, and interactive controls.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for any animations added to study flows.
- Ensure handwriting and quiz modes remain usable with keyboard and screen readers where feasible.
